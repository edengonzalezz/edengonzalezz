## Hello! ⋆.✦

<br/>


My name is Eden Gonzalez and I am pursuing my Masters in Environmental Data Science at the Bren School of Environmental Science & Management at UC Santa Barbara. Previously, I contributed to projects that span topics and geography such as: air quality in the Bay Area, cultural change in American estuaries, and soil health in Oaxaca, Mexico. During my time at Bren, I aim to continue to blend complex socio-ecological questions with robust quantitative tools.

Outside of work, I enjoy baking, rollerskating, and photography. Say hi at edengonzalez@ucsb.edu!
